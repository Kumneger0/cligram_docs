# Stories

Cligram allows you to browse and view Telegram stories anonymously without notifying the author.

---

### Anonymous Story Viewing (Ghost Mode)

By default, Cligram keeps story read receipts disabled (`readStories: false`). When you view a user's story through Cligram:
- The story author is **never notified** that you viewed their story.
- Your view is not counted in their viewer list.
- You can toggle this behavior at any time in the **Ghost Mode Settings Modal** (`F2` or `,`).

---

### Navigation & Viewing

- **Open Stories Modal:** Press **`alt + s`** from anywhere in the client.
- **Story Badge:** A count displayed in the top header of the sidebar indicates how many contacts currently have active stories.
- **Select User:** Use **↑ / k** and **↓ / j** to navigate through contacts who posted stories.
- **View Stories:** Press **Enter** on a contact to view their stories in sequence.
- **Close Modal:** Press **Esc** to dismiss the stories overlay and return to your active conversation.
